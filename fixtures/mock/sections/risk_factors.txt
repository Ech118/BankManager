FORM 10-K, FISCAL YEAR 2025 - ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Our largest ten customers accounted for 31% of fiscal 2025 revenue, and the loss of any one of them could materially reduce our revenue and operating margin.

Approximately 22% of our cost of revenue is denominated in euros. A sustained strengthening of the euro against the U.S. dollar would compress our gross margin.

Competitors bundling software with hardware at reduced prices may force us to lower prices in the mid-market segment, which could reduce our gross margin by up to 150 basis points.

We carry $1.2 billion of debt, of which $400 million matures in fiscal 2027. We may need to refinance this debt on less favorable terms.
